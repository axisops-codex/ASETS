Delete your ASETS account and data

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ASETS and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon on the dashboard).
  3. Scroll to Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Export your CSV first if you want a copy (Tax screen → Export CSV for accountant).

If you no longer have the app

Email ops@axisbsolutions.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my ASETS account". We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days, usually within a few working days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your business profile, bank and service details
  • All clients, invoices and expenses
  • All receipt images you uploaded

Everything above is removed from the live database straight away. Encrypted backups are overwritten on a rolling 30-day cycle. We keep nothing else — there is no advertising or analytics profile to delete.
