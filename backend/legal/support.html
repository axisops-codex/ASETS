Common questions

Does ASETS file my Self Assessment?

No. It gives you a running estimate of what to set aside and a CSV your accountant can use. You (or your accountant) still file with HMRC.

Which tax rates does it use?

England & Northern Ireland rates: personal allowance £12,570 (tapered above £100,000), 20% / 40% / 45% bands, plus Class 4 National Insurance at 6% and 2%. No VAT is applied — the app is built for VAT-exempt healthcare work.

Why does the receipt scan sometimes fail?

Blurry or cropped photos are the usual cause. Retake it flat, in good light, with the total visible. You can always add the expense by hand.

How do I get my data out?

Tax screen → Export CSV for accountant. Invoices can also be shared as PDFs from the invoice detail screen.
