**Full description (4000 chars max)**
```
ASETS is bookkeeping for self-employed people who find bookkeeping exhausting.

Most finance apps assume you enjoy admin. ASETS assumes the opposite. One screen tells you what you earned, what you owe and what is actually yours to keep — no spreadsheets, no jargon, no thirty-field forms.

WHAT IT DOES

• Live tax estimate — Income Tax and Class 4 National Insurance for the UK tax year (6 April to 5 April), recalculated every time you add an invoice or an expense. You always know what to set aside.
• Invoices in under a minute — pick a client, pick a service, done. Numbering is automatic, your business and payment details fill themselves in, and you can share the PDF by email or WhatsApp straight from your phone.
• Expenses without the shoebox — photograph a receipt and the amount, date, merchant and category are read for you. Or type it in; it takes seconds either way.
• Payments you can see — who owes you what, in one list, with one tap to mark a payment received.
• Companies House lookup — start typing a client's company name and the registered address fills itself in.
• Export for your accountant — a clean CSV of income, expenses and the summary, for any date range.

BUILT FOR ADHD

• Large cards, plain words, no clutter.
• A dashboard you can cut down to only the cards you care about.
• Every number visible where you need it — no drilling through menus.
• Dark mode, light mode, or follow your phone.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

• Lock the app with Face ID or your fingerprint.
• No adverts. No analytics. No tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data.
• Delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, whenever you like.

WHO IT IS FOR

Self-employed practitioners in the UK — psychologists, therapists, coaches, consultants — who invoice a handful of clients and file their own Self Assessment. The tax engine covers England and Northern Ireland rates and assumes no VAT is charged (VAT-exempt healthcare work).

IMPORTANT

ASETS gives you an estimate, not tax advice, and it does not file anything with HMRC. Check the figures with your accountant before you rely on them.
```
